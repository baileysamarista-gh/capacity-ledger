Transparent method

Every case-capacity number has a visible driver.

Capacity Ledger splits Total Workload Hours between Labeller and Checker using the Labeller Workload % column, converts each share to Effective Hours Capacity with its own hours ratio, turns that into case capacity, then scales the result by how much of the headcount (Nesting + Production) is actually production-ready.

01
Total workload hours × Labeller Workload % (and its complement) splits time between Labeller and Checker.
02
Workload hours × the effective-hours ratio (Labeller 0.4:8, Checker 0.8:8) gives Effective Hours Capacity.
03
Effective hours × 3600 ÷ effective seconds/case gives case capacity, scaled by Effective ÷ Active Headcount (utilization).

Transparent method

Every staffing number has a visible driver.

Capacity Ledger converts your forecasted Labeller and Checker cases into required hours, then required headcount — and checks it against the Active and Production headcount already on the capacity plan.

01
Forecasted cases × effective seconds/case ÷ 3600 gives required hours.
02
Required hours ÷ work hours per HC/day = Required Headcount.
03
Required Headcount is checked against Scheduled (Active) and Actual (Production + ramped Nesting) Headcount from the capacity plan.
